# Changelog

## v3.0.0 — public source preview

- GPL-3.0-only firmware with preserved NerdMiner and third-party notices.
- Clock, live mining statistics, device information, and touchscreen settings.
- Branded phone setup for Wi-Fi, Bitcoin address, pool, and timezone.
- One mining profile on battery and USB, with thermal and low-battery protection.
- Battery Saver pauses mining and Wi-Fi after three minutes of inactivity on battery.
- Cooperative light sleep, responsive wake controls, and fresh hashrate after reconnecting.
- Battery-reading startup animation and guarded percentage updates.
- Three-page Block Found celebration, reward details, and explorer QR code.
- Optional developer-supplied music; the default build is silent.
- Checksummed statistics, retryable settings saves, and two-button setup shortcut.
- Pinned build dependencies, portable tests, and source/dependency export tools.

See [release notes](docs/RELEASE_v3.0.0.md) for controls and compatibility.
Earlier internal development labels are not public release versions.
